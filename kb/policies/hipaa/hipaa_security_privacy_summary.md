# HIPAA Security and Privacy Summary

## Section 164.308 Administrative Safeguards

Covered entities and business associates must implement administrative safeguards including security management processes, assigned security responsibility, workforce security, access management, security awareness training, incident procedures, contingency planning, and evaluation.

## Section 164.312 Technical Safeguards

Technical safeguards must protect electronic protected health information through access controls, audit controls, integrity controls, authentication, and transmission security.

## Section 164.502 Uses and Disclosures

Protected health information may be used or disclosed only as permitted or required. Business associate agreements should restrict use and disclosure, require safeguards, and obligate reporting of unauthorized use or disclosure.
