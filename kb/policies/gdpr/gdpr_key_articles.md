# GDPR Key Articles

## Article 5 Principles Relating to Processing of Personal Data

Personal data must be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. It must be collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes and limited to what is necessary for those purposes. Personal data must be accurate, kept no longer than necessary, and protected with appropriate security.

## Article 6 Lawfulness of Processing

Processing is lawful only where a valid lawful basis applies, including consent, contractual necessity, legal obligation, vital interests, public task, or legitimate interests. Contracts involving personal data should identify the applicable lawful basis where relevant.

## Article 13 Information to Be Provided When Personal Data Are Collected

Data subjects must receive clear information about the controller, processing purposes, lawful basis, recipients, retention period, rights, and complaint mechanisms at the time personal data is collected.

## Article 17 Right to Erasure

Data subjects have the right to request erasure of personal data in defined circumstances. Contracts with processors should support deletion workflows and downstream deletion obligations.

## Article 28 Processor Obligations

Processing by a processor must be governed by a contract or other legal act. The contract must define subject matter, duration, nature, purpose, data categories, data subject categories, confidentiality, security measures, subprocessor controls, audit support, deletion or return of data, and assistance to the controller.

## Article 32 Security of Processing

Controllers and processors must implement appropriate technical and organizational measures. Measures may include encryption, confidentiality, integrity, availability, resilience, restoration capability, and regular testing of security controls.

## Article 44 Transfers to Third Countries or International Organisations

Transfers of personal data outside the applicable jurisdiction require an approved transfer mechanism and enforceable safeguards. Contracts should identify transfer locations, subprocessors, and applicable safeguards.
