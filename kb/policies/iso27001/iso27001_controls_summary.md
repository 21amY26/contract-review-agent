# ISO27001 Control Summary

## Control A.5 Information Security Policies

Information security policies should be defined, approved, published, communicated, and reviewed at planned intervals or when significant changes occur.

## Control A.8 Asset Management

Information assets should be identified, inventoried, assigned owners, classified, and handled according to business value, legal requirements, sensitivity, and criticality.

## Control A.15 Supplier Relationships

Supplier access to organizational assets should be governed by risk-based agreements. Supplier contracts should address information security requirements, monitoring rights, incident notification, data handling, and termination obligations.
