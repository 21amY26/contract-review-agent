# Data Privacy Policy

## Policy 1 Data Minimization

Personal data collected or processed by the company must be limited to what is necessary for a documented business purpose. Contracts should not permit vendors to use personal data for unrelated purposes.

## Policy 2 Data Subject Rights

Contracts involving personal data must require reasonable assistance for access, deletion, correction, objection, restriction, portability, and other applicable data subject requests.

## Policy 3 Cross-Border Transfers

Contracts must identify countries where personal data may be processed and require approved safeguards for restricted transfers.
