# Vendor Management Policy

## Policy 1 Vendor Due Diligence

All vendors handling company confidential information, production systems, or regulated data must complete risk-based due diligence before contract execution. Due diligence may include security questionnaires, privacy review, financial review, sanctions screening, and review of independent audit reports.

## Policy 2 Contract Security Requirements

Vendor agreements must include confidentiality obligations, data protection commitments, incident notification timelines, access restrictions, audit or assurance rights, subprocessor controls, and termination assistance where applicable.

## Policy 3 Ongoing Monitoring

Critical and high-risk vendors must be reviewed at least annually. Material changes in services, subprocessors, data location, or security posture require reassessment.
