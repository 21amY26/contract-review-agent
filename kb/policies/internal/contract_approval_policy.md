# Contract Approval Policy

## Policy 1 Approval Thresholds

Contracts with annual value above approved thresholds, regulated data processing, unusual liability terms, or non-standard payment commitments require legal and business owner approval before signature.

## Policy 2 High-Risk Terms

Unlimited liability, broad indemnities, unilateral termination rights, automatic renewal without notice, weak confidentiality, and missing data protection terms must be escalated to legal review.

## Policy 3 Records and Retention

Executed contracts and approval records must be stored in the approved repository. Contract owners must maintain renewal dates, termination windows, and key obligations.
